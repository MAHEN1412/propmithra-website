What agents say

Questions agents ask us

Who can join PropMithra?

Real estate agents and brokers working in Hyderabad and Telangana. Your account is reviewed before you can submit or view listings.

Do agents contact each other directly?

No. PropMithra contacts interested agents first and coordinates with the Property Partner.

Does PropMithra verify property title?

No. We review submitted details and documents for completeness. Title checks should be done by the buyer or tenant with a qualified professional.

Do I have to share my customer's details?

No. You can show interest without sharing your customer's personal details.

How does commission work?

It is set out in a written agreement and depends on the deal actually closing. Commission is not guaranteed.

Can I use PropMithra on my phone?

Yes. Use the Android app or this website.
