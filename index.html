How it works

From sign-up to signed deal in four steps.

  1. Join the network

    Sign up free with Google, email or your mobile number.

  2. Build your profile

    Add your company, localities and specialities. Complete KYC to list properties.

  3. Share inventory or requirements

    Post listings with photos and choose exactly who can see them.

  4. Collaborate and track the deal

    Get matched, agree terms with the other broker and follow the deal to closing.
